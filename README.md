# Employee Attrition Prediction and Workforce Analytics
### A Machine Learning and Business Intelligence Based Framework

## 📌 Overview

Employee attrition is a major challenge for organizations because unexpected employee turnover can increase recruitment costs, reduce productivity, and affect overall business performance.

This project presents a **Machine Learning and Business Intelligence based framework** for predicting employee attrition and analyzing workforce trends. The system combines predictive analytics with interactive business intelligence dashboards to help organizations identify employees who are at higher risk of leaving and understand the key factors influencing attrition.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Predict whether an employee is likely to leave the organization.
- Identify the major factors contributing to employee attrition.
- Analyze workforce patterns using exploratory data analysis.
- Build an interactive dashboard for HR and management insights.
- Provide data-driven insights to support employee retention strategies.

## 🔍 Key Features

- 📊 **Exploratory Data Analysis**
- 🤖 **Employee Attrition Prediction**
- 📈 **Workforce Analytics**
- 🧠 **Feature Importance Analysis**
- 📋 **Interactive Business Intelligence Dashboard**
- 🎯 **Risk-based Employee Segmentation**
- 💡 **Data-driven HR Insights**
